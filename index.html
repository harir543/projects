Our story

Churned by hand, kept cold the whole way.

Frost & Swirl started in 2019 with one secondhand churn, a tiny corner kitchen, and a stubborn rule: nothing leaves the freezer unless we'd eat it ourselves.

Every flavor is made in 40-pint batches with local dairy, ripe fruit and zero shortcuts — then packed with dry ice the second you order, so the last scoop tastes like the first.
